Privacy Policy

Masala Meter · last updated October 2026

Masala Meter is a personal, non-commercial movie-recommendation site/app built for a small group of friends. This page explains what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app does not have accounts, logins, or sign-up — there is no name, email, or phone number collected anywhere.

What's stored on your device

The app caches the public movie/song catalog (titles, scores, posters) in your device's local storage so it loads faster the next time you open it. This is not sent anywhere and is specific to your device — clearing the app's storage removes it.

What the app talks to

  • A backend (Google Apps Script) that serves the public movie/song catalog — the same data anyone using the site sees.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) and Google Search/Gemini, used server-side to fetch posters, cast info, and reviews — this happens on our backend, not from your device directly.

None of these exchanges include anything that identifies you personally. If you add or search for a movie, that text is used only to look up the movie — it isn't tied to your identity or stored against you.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics/tracking libraries, and doesn't sell or share data with third parties, because there is no personal data to share.
